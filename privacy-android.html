Hook the Star — Privacy Policy

Developer: Aiwa Int
Contact: aiwainit2023@gmail.com
Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy covers the Hook the Star app for Android (package com.aiwaint.hookthestar).

1. Data we collect

Advertising. The app shows optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch one for coins) and occasional full-screen ads between levels, served by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and (with your consent) personalize ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (for approximate location), device and app information and ad interactions, under Google's own privacy policy. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time: Privacy & Terms (in the level menu) → AD PRIVACY. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Analytics. The app uses GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played and to improve it. It records gameplay events (levels started, won and failed, coins earned and spent, errors) together with your device model, operating system, app version, approximate country derived from your IP address and a random identifier generated on your device. Analytics data is not linked to your name or any account, and is not used for advertising.

  • No account, name, email or sign-in is ever requested.
  • No SDKs other than Google AdMob and GameAnalytics are included.
  • The only permissions are internet access and the advertising ID (added by AdMob).

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (unlocked levels, stars, coins, outfits, sound on/off) are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage, so you keep your place between sessions. This game data never leaves your device.

3. Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → Hook the Star → Storage → Clear data. This removes everything the app has stored, including the analytics identifier and your ad consent choice. To ask for deletion of analytics data already sent, write to the contact below.

4. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

5. Changes

If a future version changes how data is processed, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
