Hook the Star — Privacy Policy

Developer: Aiwa Int
Contact: aiwainit2023@gmail.com
Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy covers the Hook the Star app for Android (package com.aiwaint.hookthestar).

1. Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal or device data.

  • No account, name, email or sign-in is ever requested.
  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs are included.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own and requests no special permissions.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (unlocked levels, stars, coins, outfits, sound on/off) are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage, so you keep your place between sessions. This data never leaves your device.

3. Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → Hook the Star → Storage → Clear data. This removes everything the app has stored.

4. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. Since it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

5. Changes

If a future version adds features that process data (for example, advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
