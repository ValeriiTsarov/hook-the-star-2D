Hook the Star — Privacy Policy

Developer: Aiwa Int
Contact: aiwainit2023@gmail.com
Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy covers the Hook the Star app for Android (package com.aiwaint.hookthestar).

1. Data we collect

Anonymous gameplay analytics only. The app uses GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played and to improve it. It records gameplay events (levels started, won and failed, coins earned and spent, errors) together with your device model, operating system, app version, approximate country derived from your IP address and a random identifier generated on your device. This data is not linked to your name or any account, and is not used for advertising.

  • No account, name, email or sign-in is ever requested.
  • No advertising SDKs and no analytics other than GameAnalytics are included.
  • The app requests no special permissions.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (unlocked levels, stars, coins, outfits, sound on/off) are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage, so you keep your place between sessions. This game data never leaves your device.

3. Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → Hook the Star → Storage → Clear data. This removes everything the app has stored, including the analytics identifier. To ask for deletion of analytics data already sent, write to the contact below.

4. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children; the analytics above are anonymous.

5. Changes

If a future version adds features that process data (for example, advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
